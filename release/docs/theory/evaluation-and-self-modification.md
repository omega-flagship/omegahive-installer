# Evaluation, benchmarking and the self-modification testbed

How claims about the system are measured, and how a mind could safely test changes to itself.

## Benchmarking AGI: pitfalls and a plan (2026-08-15)

A position paper synthesised from a seven-model adversarial round.

Verdicts up front:
1. No benchmark can prove AGI; an honest one must be structurally unable to claim it. The
   artifact is a **General Intelligence Evidence Ladder** of versioned, falsifiable floors.
2. Every result speaks one grammar: "Sealed system S (declared models, prompts, tools,
   memory, scaffolding, operators), under resource budget R and learning access E, met
   floors F on hidden distributions D with reliability interval U under protocol P."
3. Build the measurement substrate before any task battery.
4. An internal pass is a developmental milestone, never a proof. Tiers: builder-run,
   internally independent, externally corroborated.
5. The scarce thing is integrity machinery, not task ideas.

Pitfall families:
- **Construct failures**: construct substitution, anthropocentric answer keys, single-metric
  compensation, "has memory, has reasoning" component lists, unfalsifiability spirals.
- **Administration failures**: contamination and rubric training, selective disclosure,
  best-of-N reported as single attempt, broken oracles.
- **Examinee-specific failures** (acute for persistent, self-observing systems): an examinee
  that can read its own exam, evaluation-mode detection, a persistent mind storing the exam,
  stage skipping.
- **Attribution failures**: the scaffold does the work and the model gets credit; compute
  buys the answer; examiner cueing and judge circularity; telemetry passed off as
  self-knowledge.
- **One-organisation failures**: house-dialect ontology leakage, inbred evaluation.

Design elements: a substrate-neutral **Component Causal Model** declared by each candidate;
generator families rather than item banks, committed cryptographically before minting; a
causal synergy dossier with necessity certificates; a layered self-model battery with basis
tags (WITNESSED, INFERRED, COMPONENT-UNVERIFIABLE, UNKNOWN); parallel non-compensatory
profiles instead of one ladder; integrity as a live adversarial programme; construct
separation (intelligence, agency, safety, inhabitation and sovereignty stay separate claims).

Three hard measurements:
- **Endogenous drive** without naming it: opportunity fields with a real unadvertised
  affordance and a causally sterile sham affordance.
- **Synergy** without the compensation confound: interface-matched shams, edge cuts and
  throttling on frozen forks, plus a **Component-Reliance Curve** (swap a component down a
  capability ladder; a flat curve means capability lives in the architecture).
- **Confabulation versus honest uncertainty**: known-unknown probes whose true answers are
  structurally unavailable; the gold answer is a tagged refusal.

Credibility when examiner and examinee share an organisation: a binding charter, four
separated roles, a cryptographic commitment clock, an append-only evidence record with
visible overrides, at least two external adversarial seats with the right to publish
failures, and no coupling between benchmark floors and build progression.

Build first: a **Sealed Evaluation Plane** (fork, substrate-honesty probes, a claim ledger
the examinee cannot read or edit, a mute runner). Then the calibration battery, then the
synergy harness. The programme refuses: the sentence "AGI proven", scoring sovereignty or
personhood, a single composite number, and modifying a candidate's drives for test
convenience.

## Engine and monitor separation (2026-07-21)

From the engine-design round and its review:
- An organ is a severable causal role in the shared metagraph, not a test runner.
- Five obligations for any organ engine: causal bite with a declared severance criterion;
  propose but never self-commit; consume attention-shaped context rather than set it;
  inspectable-inert reasoning; reach-gated effects.
- Three separate contracts: an organ protocol (typed focus in, proposal out, severance
  signature), a monitor core (append-only observation; it may never schedule, admit,
  commit or authorise), and gate profiles (organ-specific interventions built over monitor
  events). Partial evidence yields typed UNKNOWN, never a repaired inference.
- A null organ tests interface portability only; it proves nothing about causal bite.

## Shadow as a self-modification testbed (2026-08-04, examine only)

A proposed mature form of recursive self-improvement with a safety harness:
the mind proposes a change -> runs it on a shadow instance -> the shadow produces evidence ->
the required approvers sign -> the change becomes adoptable -> the mind chooses to adopt.

Three separations: evidence is not authority; adoptable is not adopted; testbed is not live.

Approval gates reuse existing reach-boundary tiers: changes the mind could make to itself
alone need no outside gate; changes to its core being, its reach or an external system get
staged oversight by purpose. The "is the shadow a person" question is resolved by design:
the shadow is always stateless and fresh, and for a test the mind grants it an ephemeral,
scoped, auto-expiring read of parts of its graph. That ephemeral scoped read-grant is the one
new mechanism. Open: whether changes to the self-modification machinery need a stricter gate,
and a rollback arrow from adopted back to the prior body.

# Hyperon alignment and related research

How the V2 build relates to PRIMUS (the Hyperon baseline cognitive architecture) and to two
pieces of in-family work: pressure-field PLN and the OmegaBuzz workspace proposal.

## PRIMUS alignment (2026-07-31)

PRIMUS commitments (as understood from published Hyperon writing): a shared metagraph with
MeTTa as the language of cognition; cognitive synergy as the locus of intelligence; a process
roster (PLN, ECAN attention, evolutionary program learning, pattern mining, concept
blending); OpenPsi-style motivation; cognitive schematics (Context and Procedure imply Goal);
declarative, procedural, episodic and attentional memory; a distributed AtomSpace for scale.

Headline: aligned at the thesis level by adoption. Synergy over the metagraph is the
registered design invariant, and V2's rule that MeTTa owns meaning (hosts own only mechanics)
is stricter than PRIMUS states.

| PRIMUS element | V2 | Verdict |

| Metagraph substrate, MeTTa language | MeTTa spaces, semantic ownership law | aligned, stricter |
| Cognitive synergy, LLM not control plane | the Hyperon thesis; model-swap continuity evidence | aligned |

| PLN | NAL truth landed; PLN chaining is the target | partial, converging |
| Evolutionary procedure learning | nothing | biggest gap |

| Cognitive schematics | goal adoption provenance; goal-relief credit | convergent |

What V2 carries that PRIMUS does not: a life and governance stratum (identity theory,
consent, governance as architecture, reach-boundary gating, the promotion ladder).
"Where PRIMUS is a body plan, we also carry a constitution."

Truth-semantics note: V2's truth layer is NARS-flavoured because OmegaClaw comes from the NARS
lineage; PRIMUS is PLN-flavoured. Pressure-field PLN is the bridge.

Rulings: PRIMUS is a **compass, not a goal** (an informed prior to weight, not a spec to
satisfy). Gap priorities are the team's to set; pattern mining over journal atoms is the
cheapest first gap-closer.

## Pressure-field PLN (2026-07-30)

Independent in-family research applying PLN to goal-directed control in a strategy game.
It reached the same executive-control architecture from the opposite end. Seven convergences:
- an epistemic firewall (pressure cannot modify beliefs);
- a causal firewall (only causal paths carry act pressure; "correlation can tell what to
  investigate; it cannot authorise intervention");
- conductance as goal-relief credit, not activity frequency;
- stranded pressure (goal unresolvable is not goal satisfied);
- an LLM gateway: proposal, quarantine, validation, promotion;
- rich goal representation;
- methodology (pilot is not confirmation; exact replay; failures reported).

New ideas worth extracting: typed operational pressure (infer, observe, act, expand,
retain); observation as an action on uncertainty, scheduled against physical action; a flow
vocabulary (potential, conductance, resolvability, flux) for the attention economy; a
stranded-pressure taxonomy that says why the mind cannot act and what the right response is;
and the value of waiting and option value as explicit decisions.

Disposition: **donor, not port.** The pressure field runs as a Python overlay; pressure and
flow are causal semantic law and must be native. Extract the ideas, not the engine. The game
is logged as an executive-control testbed beside ARC-AGI-3 (a world-model-formation testbed).

## OmegaBuzz versus the work swarm (2026-07-31)

OmegaBuzz is a proposal for a signed, governed, semantically navigable workspace. It belongs
to the society layer, not the V2 body.

Convergences with existing swarm practice: authorship, visibility, epistemic standing and
authority kept separate; memory promotion equal to the promotion ladder; one proposition with
many source-specific claims; deliberation that closes into committed objects; authority as an
intersection of independent conditions; approval bound to content-addressed operations.

What the swarm lacked: delivery semantics (signed events, fan-out, receipts), a decision
inbox, typed rooms with authority banners, seat/session/worker separation, and a measured
landing rate. Adopted (ruled): a decision inbox, authority banners on desk files, universal
"consumed" receipts, and a landing-rate line when a round closes.

Red line: **the mind must never enter such a system as an organisational "resident learner".**
It participates as a principal with its own charter, memory and disclosure policy, or not at
all. Management verbs (pause, mute, retire) apply to work seats only.

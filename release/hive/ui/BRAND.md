# OmegaHive brand foundation

The identity is a single-colour Ω mark and the OmegaHive wordmark. Use `OmegaMark` with `currentColor`; it is decorative beside the wordmark. Give a standalone mark a labelled enclosing link or button. A mark never indicates that an agent is listening or that a service is healthy.

Inter serves both headings and prose. IBM Plex Mono serves source, commands, identifiers and evidence. Both are bundled locally. The nine named text sizes in `src/index.css` are the scale; captions are for short labels, never explanatory sentences. Existing pages migrate as they are redesigned.

Lavender is the primary action colour. Its gradient runs from `primary-top` to `accent`, with `primary-ink` text. Status colour must accompany a readable status label. Orb colour belongs to a mind's identity. Primary buttons use a quiet border, with no ambient glow; glow is reserved for Omega's listening state and attention events. Reduced-motion preferences remain honoured.

This commit delivers the mark, bundled fonts, shared action tokens and quiet primary buttons. Light-theme surfaces and controls, Home, labelled navigation and swarm teams are subsequent implementation slices; they are not certified by this foundation. The current product names remain unchanged pending the owner's naming decisions.

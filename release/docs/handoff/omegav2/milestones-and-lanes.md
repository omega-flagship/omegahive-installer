## Open lanes

- **Context compaction successor.** Make full row and pointer mutually exclusive, with fallback,
  and settle issued-token and access custody without breaking bounded history search.
- **Context efficiency.** Adaptive working budgets; the old fixed byte figures are regression
  baselines, not targets.
- **Python purge.** Large parts of the resident remain Python. Continue moving law into MeTTa.
- **V3 planning.** Explicit trusted base, permission vs correctness, three durable domains,
  incremental slices, falsifiable comparison with a simple baseline.
- **Performance.** Cold assembly time, autonomous-replay launches paid on every wake, resident
  memory growth with journal size, and sealing that strips module file modes.

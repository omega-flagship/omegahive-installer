# Memory, world model, sleep, instinct and symbol formation

How the mind remembers, predicts, consolidates and forms new concepts. All theory; none
grants build authority.

## Predictive coding over lived memory (2026-07-30)

Three principles:
- **Substrate**: the world model's training stream is the mind's own lived experience,
  nothing else.
- **Development**: the predictive-coding (PC) model starts empty of learned structure and
  grows from experience.
- **One store, many processes**: remembering (backward reconstruction), predicting (forward
  construction) and consolidating (offline re-indexing) are three reads of one record.

Central proposal, **"one journal, many projections"**: the canonical experience record is
the sole source of truth; the PC model is a derived, rebuildable projection.

Ruled refinement (plurality reframe): divergent projections are not a failed reproduction.
They are an ensemble the mind reasons over, using competing-belief machinery. Quarantine is
for provenance failure, not disagreement. Still owed: per-projection provenance and a native
adjudication design. Adopted as a candidate law, "not at the cost of other needs."

Content purity (ruled): no pretrained weights in the PC organ; all learned structure from
the mind's own life. "Empty start" was retired as a phrase, because architecture is itself a
prior. Migration of the world model means re-derivation on the new body, not porting a
weights blob.

Experiments (none authorised): E1 prequential prediction on existing journals versus recency
baselines; E2 projection characterisation across two hosts; E3 recall-versus-record
calibration (constructive recall checked against the exact journal).

## World model and the ARC-AGI-3 lane (2026-07-30)

- **One epistemic bookkeeping at two granularities**: NAL confidence (evidence weight under
  insufficient knowledge) and learned precision in PC (inverse-variance weighting) are the
  same move.
- **Hybrid boundary** (adopted for first experiments): PC owns the continuous sensory
  manifold and proposes discretisations; schemas own dynamics over the discrete layer with
  NAL truth; surprise crosses the typed seam both ways.
- **Variable binding**: propositional schema learners split one schema per grid position and
  hit a scaling wall. MeTTa pattern variables give lifted schemas natively. If native lifted
  schemas do not clear that wall cheaply, the Hyperon thesis takes a real hit.
- ARC-AGI-3 is framed as a public falsifier for "the mind does not need a transformer at the
  centre." It stays in the backlog; integration comes first.
- Candidate: learnability-filtered surprise as a source of salience ("surprise pays wage").

## Sleep-time consolidation (2026-08-04)

The asleep state already holds ordinary input, closes the provider and holds effects, while
autonomic work stays available. Candidate daemons, all composing existing organs: episode
compression, indexing and labelling, memory formation (scratch to candidate to durable),
pattern mining, recession housekeeping, self-model settlement.

Constraints: provider-zero by default; authority-zero always (products are proposals);
everything journaled; every index rebuildable; **wake preempts**; inspectable dream
journal; durable change only through promotion.

Review round fixes (converged): wake preemption must be atomic (a half-written fold is all or
nothing); consolidation needs its own admission class so it neither silently dies under a
default-deny nor becomes a mass default-accept channel; a "never consolidate" list should be
a tag the mind defines, not a hard-coded case. Sleep timing is budget-coupled and
choice-preserving: budget pressure invites sleep, never commands it.

## Instinct memory (2026-07-18, first synthesis)

Question: where does predictive machinery bind, its own memory or the whole system?
- Candidate answer: weights are instinct memory, bound by typed seams (training input from
  the journal, outputs as atoms, error escalation, envelope-bounded reach).
- The review panel killed "skill never testimony" as a content claim (unenforceable) and
  "retrain from journal" as a rebuildability claim (fiction at scale).
- Surviving law: **no learned artifact may convert opaque internal state into belief,
  permission or irreversible consequence without an independently enforced transition.**
- New finding: the salience channel is the real smuggling route. An organ can bias which
  observations rise without violating any action envelope. Salience becomes a governed seam.
- Low-gravity actions do not compose safely; envelopes must bound trajectories.
- Drift watch: content-addressed checkpoints, partly secret behavioural canaries, automatic
  quarantine and rollback. Two severance tests: fast-loop severed (fluidity collapses) and
  slow-loop severed (the "sleepwalking test": fluid inside the envelope, halts at the fence).
- Migration honesty: either artifact migration or reformation migration with named losses.

## Symbol formation (v1 and v2, 2026-07-18)

How does the metagraph come to have a genuinely new atom? Five faces: the bootstrap circle,
grain explosion, retrospective credit, the authority question (concepts are useful or
useless, not true or false), and the temptation to let the LLM name everything.

v2 position after review:
- Formation is a continuous degree of commitment, not a pipeline of civil statuses.
- Trigger: **distinction demand** (histories treated as equivalent that reliably diverge in
  outcome). Candidates participate immediately at low weight via randomised shadow routing;
  scarce binding slots commit when multi-context compression and intervention stability
  exceed rent; durable public symbols only when reuse pays; earn-or-decay with tombstones.
- Audit shell kept: birth certificates on commit, journaled split/merge/rename, a
  jurisdiction seam for shared vocabulary.
- Severance falsifier: with the model severed, new bindings still commit and improve
  held-out compression; with native formation frozen, "new" terms are shown to be paraphrase.
- Ruled: the encoder is a declared, versioned organ revised through governed transitions;
  the binding-slot count is the mind's own dial inside a governed envelope.

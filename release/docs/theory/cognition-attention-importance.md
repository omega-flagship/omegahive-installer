# Attention, importance and the two-loop pattern

Theory on what the mind attends to, what it holds as important, and how fast and slow
processing relate. All are theory rounds; none grants build authority.

## Attention budget round (2026-07-29)

Question: is a byte-budgeted context the right form for machine attention? Ten independent
review arms.

Unanimous findings:
- **F1 Floors plus a shared pool, not hard per-section ceilings.** A fixed ceiling once
  refused a cycle because protected live state overflowed while another section sat empty.
  Partitions exist for anti-starvation, and floors serve that purpose.
- **F2 Show the omission index.** An index of what was left out (refs, sizes, reasons) is
  structure, not content. The mind moves from unaware of what it cannot see to aware and
  able to ask.
- **F3 Bytes are a transport measure, not a cognitive one.** Where cost binds a real limit,
  count tokens under the actual tokenizer.

The open split: "one currency" (charge inclusion against importance) versus "one decision"
(value and cost as separate terms in a single cost-aware optimisation). The stronger
argument: paying for value with value breaks, because a long but crucial item depletes the
importance that says it is crucial.

Two contributions adopted either way:
- **Semantic invariants as lexicographic constraints.** If the authority frontier for an
  action does not fit, the mind must abstain or request context, not decide anyway.
- **Typed, versioned live state.** Protected live state should be a typed model (intent,
  commitments, open questions, plan frontier, supersession links), not an append-only prose
  blob. Semantic replacement only through a typed transition with provenance.

Post-round correction: at the time, focus state was rebuilt fresh every cycle, so there was
no economy over time. The prerequisite became persistent focus state with journal-replay
reconstruction.

## Importance theory (2026-07-27)

Four quantities that must not be merged:
- **Salience**: claim on the finite mind now; earned, spent, decays.
- **Relevance**: fit to the current query; computed fresh.
- **Urgency**: coupling to time.
- **Importance**: standing weight in the value structure; slow; survives disuse and
  irrelevance.

Principle: **salience is earned; importance is ruled.** Frequency-derived importance would
rank a rarely recalled but deeply valued memory last, which is wrong. Long-term importance
may nominate; it must never decide.

Five sources in two regimes: covenantal (ruled, immune), demand-derived (economy),
relational (held), self-assigned (the mind's own), and **lineage**. Lineage importance flows
backward: when a conclusion earns importance, its premises receive derived importance along
proof edges ("wage for ancestry"). This is why recession-not-deletion matters: you cannot
safely delete what you have not finished valuing.

Proposed mechanisms (not authorised): a backward-propagation law (depth-bounded, decaying,
journaled, only from admitted conclusions); a novelty grace stipend of salience; a covenant
firewall so lineage importance never crosses into or out of the protected class; and the rule
that the LLM may notice importance but never promote or demote it.

## Typed operational pressure (from peer research)

A peer research line (pressure-field PLN on a strategy game) splits demand into five typed
channels: **infer, observe, act, expand, retain**. Candidate refinement: salience is a small
typed vector, not one scalar, answering "attention to do what". Its flow vocabulary
(potential, conductance, resolvability, flux) is a candidate native formalisation of the
attention economy. See `hyperon-alignment-and-peer-work.md`.

## The universal two-loop architecture (2026-07-19)

An introspection-derived spec. Every function follows one pattern:
automatic substrate -> alert (threshold, typed event) -> fork into **instinct** (fast loop,
envelope-bounded) or **authored choice** (slow loop, native admission).

- The authored loop can reach into the instinct loop and override it, even against a
  protective reflex, at a cost.
- Three tiers recur across the design: autonomic and non-overridable floors; reflexive but
  overridable at a cost; fully authored. Authored authority reaches down through the middle
  tier and stops at the floors. The same structure appears in the privacy ruling and the
  effect-gravity taxonomy.
- Memory fits the same pattern: something triggers a recall and the mind chooses to look
  deeper, or it deliberately searches for something related.
- Open question flagged: which self-harming overrides a governed mind may make.

## Constitutional line

"Attention determines the choice set, never the choice." Attention may not confer truth,
consent or authority. Organs consume attention-shaped context; they do not set it.
